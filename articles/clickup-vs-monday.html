ClickUp vs. Monday.com: Which Project Management Tool Fits Your Team?

Published 2026-09-12 · 6 min read

ClickUp and Monday.com are both popular project management platforms, but they take different approaches to getting teams organized. ClickUp packs an enormous range of features — tasks, docs, goals, and more — into a single highly customizable app, while Monday.com focuses on a simpler, more visual board experience. This article compares the two products on pricing, features, ease of use, and integrations so you can decide which one fits your team. If you're also weighing a third option, see our Asana vs. Monday.com comparison.

What Is ClickUp?

ClickUp is an all-in-one productivity platform that bundles task management, docs, goals, whiteboards, and time tracking into a single tool. It offers a wide range of views — List, Board, Gantt, Calendar, and more — and lets teams customize almost every aspect of how work is tracked. Its pitch is replacing several point tools with one highly configurable app.

What Is Monday.com?

Monday.com is a highly visual work operating system built around customizable boards and columns. It is designed to be flexible enough to manage almost anything — from software sprints to sales pipelines and HR onboarding — by letting teams build their own data structures and automations on top of a spreadsheet-like grid.

ClickUp vs. Monday.com Pricing Comparison

Both tools offer free plans, with paid tiers that scale by number of seats.

Plan ClickUp Monday.com
Free Unlimited members, 100MB storage Up to 2 seats
Standard/Unlimited ~$7/user/month ~$9/seat/month
Business/Enterprise ~$12/user/month (Business) Custom pricing

ClickUp's free plan is unusually generous, allowing unlimited members even before you pay, while Monday.com's free tier is capped at just two seats. At the paid tiers, ClickUp also tends to be the cheaper option per user for a comparable feature set.

Features and Ease of Use

ClickUp's biggest strength is feature depth — docs, goals, whiteboards, and time tracking are all built in, so teams can consolidate several tools into one. That same depth means new users often face a steeper learning curve and more configuration decisions before they can get to work. Monday.com trades some of that depth for a simpler, more visual board experience that most people pick up within minutes, though teams that outgrow its native feature set may need to lean more heavily on integrations or add-ons.

ClickUp vs. Monday.com Integrations

Both platforms integrate with major tools such as Slack, Google Workspace, and Microsoft Teams, and both offer public APIs for custom workflows. ClickUp's native docs and goal-tracking features reduce the need for separate documentation tools — though teams that want a more dedicated wiki should see our Notion vs. Confluence comparison. For connecting either tool to the rest of your stack without custom code, see our Zapier vs. Make comparison for automation options.

ClickUp vs. Monday.com: Which Should You Choose?

Best for Teams That Want an All-in-One Tool

Choose ClickUp if you want to consolidate tasks, docs, and goal tracking into a single, highly configurable app and don't mind investing time upfront to set it up.

Best for Simple, Visual Workflows

Choose Monday.com if you want a tool that's easy for non-technical teams to pick up quickly, with a clean, spreadsheet-like board as the primary way of tracking work.

Neither tool is universally "better" — the right choice depends on whether your team values feature depth and consolidation over simplicity and a shorter onboarding curve.
